# Destiny Builds Companion

A Destiny 2 companion: builds with full synergy breakdowns, raid guides, and a searchable database of every weapon, perk, exotic, armor set, mod and ability with community-tested numbers.

Open the site, or on your phone tap Share → Add to Home Screen to install it like an app.

Your imported gear (DIM spreadsheets or gear added by hand) is saved only in your own browser.

Perk numbers: Clarity community database. Game data: Bungie's Destiny 2 manifest. Builds credit their original creators.
Not affiliated with Bungie.
